A Taste of Congee Queen

Congee Queen serves Cantonese style food. Here are a few dishes to look for on the menu.

Wonton: A tasty dumpling dish.

Soya Sauce Chicken

Tender Cantonese style chicken seasoned with savory soya sauce. A delicious dish to enjoy with rice.

Congee

A warm rice porridge that can be enjoyed plain or with savory toppings.

Wonton Noodles

A comforting combination of noodles and wontons in a flavorful broth.

Fried Rice

Hearty Cantonese-style fried rice, a satisfying dish with savory flavors.
